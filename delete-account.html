Delete Your RoporChat Account

You can request deletion of your RoporChat account and associated personal data.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Send an email to our support address.
  2. Use the subject: RoporChat Account Deletion Request
  3. Provide the account information requested by our support team.

Contact

Email: roporchat@gmail.com

What Happens After Your Request?

We will verify the request and delete applicable account data, subject to information that may need to be retained for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or dispute resolution.

Important

Do not send passwords, OTPs, payment information, or other security credentials in your deletion request.
